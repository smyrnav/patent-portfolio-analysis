This brief answers the [central question](../01-research-design/research-design.md#central-research-question) as far as public data allows: it is an outside-in assessment, not an evaluation of Nokia's internal strategy.

## The answer in one paragraph

Nokia's publicly observable patent position looks **strongest in optical networking, clearly behind Cisco and Huawei in IP routing, and potentially most exposed in data-centre switching** — the category growing fastest because of AI infrastructure demand. On preliminary claim-based estimates, Nokia's US patent families with grant evidence number about 768 in optical (641–840), 233 in routing (151–329) and 70 in switching (46–97); once families showing another current assignee are set aside, the switching estimate falls to 26. These are priorities to investigate, not verdicts: ownership, legal status and classification recall are unverified.

## What this is not
