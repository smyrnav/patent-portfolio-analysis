# Commercial alignment

**Status: not tested.** This page answers RQ3: are there gaps or misalignments between Nokia's technology portfolio and its commercial opportunities? The inputs below are collected, and the comparison needs claim-based patent trends first.

## Commercial signals collected

| Signal | Figure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| IP Networks growth, Q3 2026, constant currency | +43% | [Nokia Q3 2026 release](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/nokia-corporation-interim-report-q3-2026-2026-10-22) |
| Optical Networks growth, Q3 2026, constant currency | +18% | Same release |
| Sales to AI and cloud customers | 12% of group sales | [Nokia Q3 report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/924613/000110465926117972/tm2628115d1_6k.htm) |
| Network Infrastructure R&D growth, Q3 2026 | +1%, against +17% sales | [Same report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/924613/000110465926117972/tm2628115d1_6k.htm) |
| Market positions by segment | [Segment figures](market-context-and-peer-selection.md#segment-figures-behind-the-map) | IDC and Dell'Oro |

## The test

| Category | Commercial signal | Patent signal needed | Possible reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Optical** | Strong public position, +18% unit growth | Claim-based family share, 2020–2024, with and without Infinera | Aligned if patent share and growth hold up |
| **IP routing** | +43% unit growth | Claim-based share of new families, 2020–2024 | Exposed if sales grow on a position that trails Cisco and Huawei |
| **Switching** | Led by others in data centre Ethernet and AI back-end | Claim-based family share, and who currently holds it | Exposed, or simply not where Nokia competes |

Each result would become one of five decisions: maintain, expand, reallocate, optimise or investigate further.

## What it can't show

Public data doesn't link patents to products, so this shows whether patent activity and business growth point the same way. It doesn't show that patents caused any growth, or that any product depends on a patent.
